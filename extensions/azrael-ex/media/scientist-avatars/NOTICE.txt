These 150 scientist portraits are user-requested AI-generated risograph artwork created with OpenAI built-in image_gen for this project. They are identity illustrations, not photographs. The user explicitly approved all 150 source artworks and authorized bundling them as 256x256 JPEG images.

The approved original PNG files remain unchanged. This bundled set was resized with System.Drawing HighQualityBicubic interpolation and encoded as JPEG at quality 90, retaining each source image's full framing. No new image was generated. Source approval hashes, source paths, conversion settings and output hashes are recorded in provenance.json; manifest.json supplies stable scientist identities and localized labels.
