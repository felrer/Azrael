These portraits are AI-generated fan art of Blue Archive characters, generated with OpenAI image_gen for this project. The collection contains the original100 student IDs and100 additional student/costume IDs. Six regenerated portraits were explicitly approved by the user and replaced without changing their IDs; the other194 PNG bytes are retained. All portraits were generated on user request and visually checked against character references and the original pixel-avatar baseline. Generation metadata, attempt records and independent review decisions are linked in provenance.json. Selected generated PNG bytes are copied unchanged.

Blue Archive and its characters belong to their respective rights holders, including NEXON Games and NEXON; applicable regional publishing rights belong to their respective publishers. This project is unofficial and is not endorsed by those rights holders. No ownership or license to the original characters or reference artwork is claimed.

The roster and identity references were obtained from SchaleDB (https://schaledb.com/data/en/students.min.json). Per-student portrait and collection reference URLs, generation metadata locations, approved hashes, and the Azusa (Swimsuit) style baseline are recorded in provenance.json. Reference artwork and the style baseline are not included in this packaged roster. The local review HTML referenced in provenance.json provides all 200 portraits with identity references, baseline comparisons and 20/32/64px display samples.
